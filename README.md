# Revenue Leakage & Profitability Analysis — Advanced Excel Project

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 5,000+ sales transactions using Microsoft Excel to understand revenue performance, profitability, customer behavior, sales channels, and regional performance.

The project focuses on identifying revenue leakage caused by discounts, product costs, shipping costs, returns, and cancellations, and converting the analysis into actionable business insights.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and profitability
- Identify major sources of revenue leakage
- Evaluate discounts, costs, returns, and cancellations
- Compare product and category performance
- Analyze customer segments
- Compare sales channels
- Evaluate regional performance
- Identify opportunities to improve profitability

## Dataset

The dataset contains 5,000 sales transactions with information about:

- Order details
- Customers
- Cities and regions
- Customer segments
- Product categories and products
- Quantity and pricing
- Discounts
- COGS
- Shipping costs
- Sales channels
- Payment modes
- Order status

## Key Results

- Total Gross Sales: approximately ₹21.21 crore
- Total Net Sales: approximately ₹18.55 crore
- Total Final Profit: approximately ₹3.08 crore
- Total Orders: 5,000
- Average Order Value: approximately ₹37,103
- Overall Profit Margin: approximately 16.6%

## Business Insights

The analysis helps identify the major revenue and profitability drivers and highlights areas where discounts, operating costs, returns, and cancellations can affect profitability.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance across products, customers, channels, and regions.
